# Market-space map — methodology and known error modes

Generated by `scripts/build_market_space.py` from the full 1,000-record
Crunchbase ODM sample (`data/crunchbase_odm_sample.json`, Apache-2.0,
fetched by `scripts/fetch_crunchbase_odm.py`). Outputs: `market_space.csv`
(one row per sector × size-band × AI-readiness cell) and `top_cells.md`.

## How cells are defined

- **Sector**: the record's first Crunchbase industry label. Single-label
  attribution over-fragments multi-category companies; "Unclassified"
  collects records with no label.
- **Size band**: Crunchbase `num_employees` collapsed to five bands
  (1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-1000, 1000+).
- **AI-readiness (0–3, proxy)**: +1 for an AI/data industry label, +1 for AI
  keywords in the company description, +1 for AI/ML tools in the BuiltWith
  tech tags. This is a *population-scale proxy*, *not* the per-lead 6-input
  scoring from the hiring-signal brief — job posts, team pages, GitHub
  activity, and executive commentary are not present in the ODM sample.
- **Bench match**: fraction of Tenacious bench engineers (from
  `bench_summary.json`, weighted by availability) whose stack keywords
  appear in the company's industry/description/tech fields.
- **Combined score** (weights in the script): population presence 25%,
  any-recent-funding 30%, readiness ≥ 1 20%, bench match 25%.

## Validation status

The challenge's own bar applies: a superficial market map is worse than none.
This map has **not** been validated against a hand-labeled sample — no
precision/recall numbers exist yet for the readiness proxy. Until a ~50-company
hand-label pass is done, treat the map as a **targeting prior** that decides
where to *point* the per-lead pipeline, never as evidence to put in front of
a prospect.

## Known error modes

- **False negatives (quietly sophisticated)**: companies doing serious AI
  work with no public labels/keywords score 0. The description field is
  marketing copy; silence is not absence.
- **False positives (loud but shallow)**: "AI" in a company description is
  cheap; a keyword hit is one point regardless of substance.
- **Funding staleness/sparsity**: only ~12% of ODM records carry a funding
  event, and the public sample is a frozen snapshot whose funding dates are
  mostly years old — so the map scores "has a recorded funding event" rather
  than the 180-day window the per-lead pipeline uses. A live pilot swaps in
  fresh Crunchbase data before trusting this dimension.
- **Sector fragmentation**: first-label attribution splits e.g. "FinTech,
  Payments, SaaS" companies across whichever label Crunchbase listed first.
- **Bench-match keyword coarseness**: stack keywords over-match generic
  terms ("api", "cloud"); the per-lead bench-to-brief gate in the pipeline
  remains authoritative.
